# Welcome to my repository 📚

Hi!

I'm currently a PhD student at the Formal Security Engineering group at MPI-SP, working on formal approaches to mitigate and systematically analyse microarchitectural side-channel attacks. Before that, I was a platform security researcher at HP Labs in Bristol, UK. I previously obtained a MSc in Computer Science and Engineering at [Politecnico di Milano](https://www.polimi.it), working on real-time systems security at the [HEAP Lab](https://heaplab.deib.polimi.it/) during my MSc thesis.
My main interests mainly revolve around OS security, trusted computing technologies, cryptography and compilers. Here you can find the projects I've been involved with during my University years, as well as some research output.

For less serious personal projects instead, head off [here](https://github.com/nbenatti).
